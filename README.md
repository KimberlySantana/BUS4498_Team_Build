### System Goal
The goal of our AI IT Help Desk Agent is to help employees quickly solve common technology problems by identifying the issue, providing troubleshooting steps, and sending more urgent issues to IT staff. Hence, increasing the resolution rate from the current rate to approximately 70%. As well as reducing by 30% the average time to solve common IT issues. Therefore, increasing satisfaction score of at least 4 out of 5, without sharing confidential information or even getting to the point where the issues need human IT support.

### Who Is Better Off When This Works?

Both employees and IT staff will be better off because employees can get more efficient help while IT staff can spend more time on urgent issues.
